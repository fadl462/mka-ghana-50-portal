02 / ELIGIBILITY & GUIDELINES

Make it original. Make it meaningful.

01

Who can enter?

MKA Ghana members, including Atfal. Atfal entries require parent or guardian consent.

02

Original work

Submit your own concept and respect other people's intellectual property.

03

Explain your idea

Give your design a title and briefly explain its inspiration and symbolism.

04

One image per entry

Upload one PNG, JPG or WebP image. PDF, SVG and multiple-image uploads are not accepted.

Dates and final rules Opening dates, deadlines, file specifications, judging criteria and intellectual property terms will be announced after approval.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps.

01

Prepare

Read the approved brief and create an original concept.

02

Submit

Complete the form and upload one image. Each entry gets its own reference.

03

Follow up

Track your entry using its reference number and email address.

You may submit more than one independent concept. Community likes are for appreciation; appointed judges determine the official results.

04 / QUICK FAQs

Good to know.

Can I submit more than one design?

Yes. There is no fixed submission cap. Submit each concept separately.

Can I change or withdraw an entry?

Submitted entries cannot be edited. You may withdraw an entry using the original browser/device and its owner withdrawal option.

How are winners selected?

Community likes do not determine the winner. Official judges assess the entries.

When does submission open?

The organisers will announce the official dates. The homepage will show when submissions are open.
