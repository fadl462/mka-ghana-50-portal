01 / ABOUT THE COMPETITION

Fifty years of faith,
brotherhood and service.

The selected design will become the visual starting point for the MKA Ghana @ 50 anniversary identity, subject to the approval of the Mother Committee.

04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few helpful answers.

Who can participate?

MKA Ghana members, including Atfal, may participate. Atfal entries require parent or guardian consent.

How many concepts can I submit?

There is no fixed submission cap in the portal. Each concept must be submitted as a separate entry with its own reference number.

Which file formats are accepted?

Each entry must contain exactly one PNG, JPG or WebP image. PDF, SVG and multiple-image uploads are not accepted. The current maximum upload size is shown on the submission form.

Can I withdraw an entry?

Yes. Use the original browser/device used to submit the entry and the owner withdrawal option on the Track Entry page. A withdrawn entry is removed from the public gallery and cannot receive further likes.

Do community likes determine the winner?

No. Likes are a way for the community to appreciate entries. The appointed judges determine the official competition results.

When does the competition close?

The official opening and closing dates will be announced after approval. The portal will indicate when submissions are open.

How do I check my submission?

Use the Track Entry page with your submission reference and email address.
