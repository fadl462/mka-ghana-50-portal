MAJLIS KHUDDAMUL AHMADIYYA50GHANA · 1977—2027HERITAGE / BROTHERHOOD / SERVICE

One milestone.
Many perspectives.

Fifty years represents generations of faith, brotherhood, service and sacrifice. This competition creates an opportunity for members to help express that journey through a distinctive anniversary logo.

The selected design will become the visual starting point for the MKA Ghana @ 50 anniversary identity, subject to the approval of the Mother Committee.
